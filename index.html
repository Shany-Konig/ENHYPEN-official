About ENHYPEN

ENHYPEN (엔하이픈) is a 7-member South Korean boy group under BE:LIFT Lab, a subsidiary label under HYBE. The group consists of the final members of the survival show I-LAND; Jungwon, Heeseung, Jay, Jake, Sunghoon, Sunoo, and Ni-ki. They debuted on November 30, 2020, with their first mini album, BORDER: DAY ONE.

ENHYPEN was formed through the reality survival show I-LAND, which aired from June 26 to September 18, 2020. The show was a collaboration between CJ ENM and Big Hit Entertainment (now HYBE Corporation). The show featured 23 trainees from various entertainment companies, including Big Hit, and aimed to create a new boy group. The final lineup was determined through a combination of viewer votes and evaluations by the show's producers.

Music

ENHYPEN has released several albums and singles since their debut, showcasing their unique sound and captivating performances. Their music often explores themes of youth, identity, and growth.

Some of their popular songs include "Given-Taken," "Drunk-Dazed," "ENHYPEN," and "Go Big or Go Home." The group has also participated in various music shows and events, gaining a strong fanbase both in South Korea and internationally.
